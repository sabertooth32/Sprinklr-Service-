# 01 · ICP scorecard — Sprinklr Service, APJI large enterprise

**Purpose:** decide in one pass whether a logo deserves a full pack.
**Rule:** score only from evidence. No evidence = 0 points for that line.

## Scorecard (100 points)

| # | Signal | What "full marks" looks like | Points |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Contact volume | ≥ 1M service interactions a year, or ≥ 500 agent seats | 20 |
| 2 | Scale | Revenue ≥ US$1B, or ≥ 5M end customers | 15 |
| 3 | Channel complexity | ≥ 5 live support channels. Must include voice + digital + social or messaging | 15 |
| 4 | Industry fit | High-contact B2C: banking, insurance, telecom, retail / e-commerce, travel, utilities, consumer tech, automotive, healthcare, public services | 10 |
| 5 | Fragmentation | ≥ 3 separate tools across voice, chat, bot, social, knowledge, QA | 10 |
| 6 | AI ambition vs. reality | Public AI / CX agenda **and** a visible gap in at least one channel | 10 |
| 7 | Why now | Trigger in the last 12 months: outage, regulator action, new CX / ops leader, cost programme, contract renewal, merger | 10 |
| 8 | APJI footprint | ≥ 2 APJI markets or ≥ 2 service languages | 5 |
| 9 | Access | Named, reachable buying centre. Bonus if already a Sprinklr customer in another product | 5 |

Partial marks: give half points when the signal is present but below the bar. Give zero when unknown.

## Tiers

| Tier | Score | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 75–100 | Full pack. Multi-thread now. |
| **B** | 55–74 | Light pack. One buying centre. Re-score in 90 days. |
| **C** | < 55 | Stop. Log reason. Do not send content. |

## Hard stops (any one = no outreach)

| Stop | Why |
|---|---|
| Fewer than 100 service agents | Deal too small for enterprise motion |
| Pure B2B, low contact volume | Weak fit for omnichannel service |
| Signed a full-suite competitor in the last 12 months, no visible gaps | Bad timing. Park and set a reminder |
| Sanctions, legal or reputational block | Compliance |
| Open Sprinklr Service opportunity already owned by an AE | Route to the owner. Do not double-prospect |

## Who we sell to (buying centres)

| Buying centre | Typical titles | What they own | What they fear |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer service / contact centre | Head of Customer Service, Contact Centre Director, Head of Customer Operations | Cost to serve, CSAT, FCR, agent attrition | Missed SLAs, rising volume, flat budget |
| CX / customer experience | Chief Customer Officer, Head of CX, Head of Customer Journey | NPS, journey quality, complaints | Broken hand-offs, public complaints |
| Digital / channels | Chief Digital Officer, Head of Digital Channels, Head of Conversational AI | App, chat, bot, self-service adoption | Low bot containment, channel sprawl |
| Technology | CIO, CTO, Head of Contact Centre Technology, Enterprise Architect | Platform, integration, security, vendor count | Tool sprawl, failed migration, AI risk |
| Operations / transformation | COO, Head of Transformation, Head of Service Excellence | Productivity, cost programme | Savings not delivered |
| Social / brand care | Head of Social Media, Head of Digital Engagement | Social response, brand risk | A viral complaint |
| Risk / compliance (influencer) | Head of Conduct, Complaints, Data Privacy | Regulator outcomes | Fines, repeat incidents |
| Procurement (gate) | Category Manager, IT / Contact Centre | Commercials, vendor risk | Overlap spend |

| Role | Meaning | SDR goal |
|---|---|---|
