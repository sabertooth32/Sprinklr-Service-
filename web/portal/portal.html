How it works, in one minute

You pick the account. AI agents do the reading and the checking. You do the human part: check, test as a customer, and send.

You get three files

  • SDR brief: for you only. Never forward it.
  • Infographic: to send to the prospect.
  • Outreach kit: messages to copy and paste.

Live tests: do

  • Act as an ordinary customer.
  • Give a first name only if asked: “Alex”.
  • Ask general questions.
  • Use your personal email and phone.

Live tests: never

  • Say which company you work for.
  • Claim an account or complaint you do not have.
  • Pass an identity check or fill in a form.
  • Record a call or name their staff.

Words you will see

ICP = how well the account fits us, out of 100. Tier A = go now. Buying centre = a department with a say. Break point = where the customer journey goes wrong. Outside-in = built from public information only.
