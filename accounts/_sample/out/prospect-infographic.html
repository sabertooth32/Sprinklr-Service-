From observation to opportunity

Three moves that lift the journey

Each one builds on what you already have. None needs a rip-and-replace.
Sprinklr Service

One conversation, from assistant to agent

What good looks like
The assistant passes the full conversation to a live agent. The customer never starts again.
How Sprinklr Service helps
Sprinklr AI Agents. Customer-facing AI agents that resolve issues autonomously on chat and voice and hand over to humans with context.

Known on every channel

What good looks like

Unified Agent Desktop. Shows customer history, CRM data and voice plus digital conversations on one agent screen.
