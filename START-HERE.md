# Start here — Account Radar in plain words

New to this? Read this page only. It takes 3 minutes.

## What this is

A helper that researches one company for you and hands you a ready-to-use sales pack for Sprinklr Service.

| You do | The helper does |
|---|---|
| Type a company name | Researches the company, its departments and its people |
| Wait (about 2–3 hours, in the background) | Checks every fact twice. Drops what it cannot prove |
| Read the brief | Builds your brief, a visual for the prospect, and ready-to-send messages |
| Do 5 quick checks, then send | Nothing. It never contacts anyone for you |

## The five steps

```
 ① Open        ② Type                 ③ Wait            ④ Read            ⑤ Check and send
 Claude on  →  /account-radar      →  Keep working   →  Open the brief  →  Five checks (page 2)
 this repo     Company Name           It tells you      Start at page 1    Then send message 1
                                      when done
```

| Step | What you do | What you should see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open Claude Code (web or desktop) and choose this repository | A chat box |
| 2 | Type `/account-radar` followed by the company name. Example: `/account-radar Qantas` | "Workflow launched in background" |
| 3 | Wait. You can close the tab and come back | A message: pack ready, with three files |
| 4 | Open **sdr-brief.pdf**. Read pages 1 to 6 first | Snapshot, your 10-day play, why now, people |
| 5 | Do the five checks on page 2. Then send the first email with **prospect-infographic.pdf** attached | Your first touch is out |

## The three files you get

| File | Who it is for | Use it to |
|---|---|---|
| `sdr-brief.pdf` | You only. Never forward | Understand the account and know what to do each day |
| `prospect-infographic.pdf` | The prospect | Attach to your first email |
| `outreach-kit.md` | You | Copy and paste emails, LinkedIn notes and call openers |

## Words you will meet

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ICP | Ideal customer profile. A score out of 100 for how well the company fits us |
| Tier A / B / C | A = go now. B = light touch. C = stop |
| Buying centre | A department that has a say in the purchase |
| Incumbent | The vendor they use today |
| Break point | A moment where their customer's journey goes wrong |
| Experience index / AI index | Two scores out of 100 for how good their service and their AI look from outside |
| Outside-in | Built only from public information. An estimate, not their internal data |
| Value at stake | A careful estimate of yearly savings. Always say "illustrative" |

## Live tests: you are "Alex", an ordinary customer

Before you send, you try the company's support yourself for a few minutes. This shows you what their customers live through.

| Do | Never |
|---|---|
| Act as a normal member of the public | Say you are from Sprinklr, or mention sales or research |
| Give a first name only if asked: "Alex" | Give a full fake identity or pass an ID check |
| Ask general questions: "How do I…?", "What should I do if…?" | Claim an account, a transaction or a complaint you do not have |
| Use your personal email and phone | Use your Sprinklr email, signature or work phone |
| Keep notes for yourself | Share screenshots or name their staff |

Full rules: `framework/08-live-test-rules.md`.

When you later email or call the prospect, you are openly an SDR from Sprinklr. That is normal and correct. Only the live tests are done as a customer.

## If something goes wrong

| You see | Do this |
|---|---|
| "Stopped: ICP gate" | The company is a weak fit. Pick another account |
| "Needs attention" | Type: `fix the pack for <company> and rebuild` |
| A contact has left the company | Skip that person. Use the next priority-1 contact |
| You are unsure what to say | Read page 2 of the brief again, or ask Claude: `explain page 7 of the brief in simple words` |
| Anything about pricing or contracts | Stop and pass it to your manager or AE |

## You can always ask in plain words

Examples you can type:

- `explain the brief for DBS Bank in simple words`
- `which three people should I contact first and why?`
- `rewrite email 1 for a Head of Digital, shorter`
- `rebuild the pack for DBS Bank`
